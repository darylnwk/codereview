Design system

Carbon ground, bone ink. One highlighter for the agent's finding, one rust for what breaks. The diff is the hero of every page; chrome stays out of its way. Tokens live in tokens.css, components in site.css.

  • No gradients, no blur. The read head is a flat 9% wash.
